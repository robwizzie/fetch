# Shelved arenas

`Game._load_dir` lists files, not folders, so anything in here is out of the arena picker while
staying in the repo. Nothing has been deleted: move a `.tres` back up one level and it returns.

Cut back to a smaller, stronger set — each remaining arena is about one idea, and these
were not yet. (Kitchen came back once it had one: its conveyor belts.) Sunflower Courtyard
and Moonlit Rooftop were symmetric prop pairs in a flat box, with palettes so low-contrast that
a bone lying on the floor disappeared into it. Their scenes are untouched under
`scenes/arenas/`, so they are a good starting point whenever they get an idea of their own.
